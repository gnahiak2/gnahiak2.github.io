02 · About

I like knowing
how things work.

I'm a Singaporean who likes taking things apart to see how they work — software, hardware, servers, networks. I write a lot of Rust and TypeScript, tinker with ESP32s, and keep a home server around for the fun of it.

When I'm not coding I'm probably makan-ing, lepaking at a hawker centre, or chasing the next "I wonder if I can…" idea. Built with code, kopi, and a healthy dose of kiasu.

Proud member of Hack Club — a community of teenagers who build things with code and curiosity. Hack Club is red, Singapore is red. Coincidence? I think not.
